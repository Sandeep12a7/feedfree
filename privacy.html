FeedFree privacy policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026
FeedFree is made by Sandeep Vaskuri. Contact: vaskurisandeep@gmail.com

FeedFree is a Chrome extension that hides the feed on YouTube, Instagram, X, Reddit, LinkedIn and Facebook. This policy explains what it stores, what it sends, and why.

The short version

  • FeedFree does not collect, sell or share your browsing, posts, messages or personal information. It has no analytics, no tracking and no ads.
  • Everything it stores stays in your browser on your computer.
  • The only thing it sends anywhere is your license key, to the seller Lemon Squeezy, to check that the key is valid.

What FeedFree looks at on the six supported sites

FeedFree runs only on these sites: www.youtube.com, www.instagram.com, x.com, www.reddit.com, old.reddit.com, www.linkedin.com and www.facebook.com. On those pages it hides parts of the page with CSS. To do that it looks at:

  • The page address (for example / or /feed/), to know whether you're on a feed page.
  • Whether a password field exists on the page, so it never hides a login form. It doesn't read what is typed into it.
  • The page's background colour, so the quote matches the site's light or dark theme.

None of this is stored or sent anywhere. FeedFree does not read the text of posts, comments or messages, and it doesn't look at any other website.

What FeedFree stores on your computer

FeedFree uses Chrome's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local). It is not synced to your Google account or sent to us. It holds:

  • Your settings: which sites are on, pauses, schedule, pages you chose never to hide, quote choices and your own quotes.
  • Today's count of feed pages hidden: today's date and a number. It resets each day.
  • When your free trial started, a single date.
  • Your license, if you activated one: the key, the activation ID Lemon Squeezy assigned, whether it's active, and when it was last checked.

Removing FeedFree from Chrome deletes all of this.

What FeedFree sends, and to whom

FeedFree contacts Lemon Squeezy (api.lemonsqueezy.com), the company that sells FeedFree, in three situations only:

  1. When you activate a license key in Settings.
  2. About once a week after that, to check the key is still valid (for example, that it hasn't been refunded).
  3. When you choose Deactivate this computer.

Each request contains your license key, the activation ID Lemon Squeezy gave this installation, and the fixed name "FeedFree on Chrome". Like any web request, it also reveals your IP address to Lemon Squeezy. Nothing about your browsing is included.

Lemon Squeezy's reply can include the name and email address used to buy the key. FeedFree does not store or use them.

If you never enter a license key, FeedFree sends nothing at all.

Buying FeedFree

Purchases happen on Lemon Squeezy's checkout page, not inside the extension. Lemon Squeezy is the merchant of record: it handles payment, tax and the receipt email with your key, under its own privacy policy. We receive the order details Lemon Squeezy shares with sellers, such as your name, email address and what you bought, and use them only to provide support and refunds.

Problem reports

Settings has a Write a report button. It opens your own email app with the site name and FeedFree's version filled in. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. We use emails only to answer them.

Permissions FeedFree asks for

  • Read and change your data on the six supported sites: needed to hide their feeds. It doesn't run on any other site.
  • Storage: to save your settings on your computer.
  • Alarms: to end a pause on time, and to schedule the weekly license check.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. It's hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy statement.

Children

FeedFree isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect any information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above, and describe the change in the release notes.
