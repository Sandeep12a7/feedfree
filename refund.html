Refund policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026

14-day money-back guarantee

You can try every FeedFree feature free for 7 days before you pay. If you buy a license and it isn't right for you, you can have a full refund within 14 days of purchase. You don't need to give a reason.

How to ask for a refund

  1. Email vaskurisandeep@gmail.com from the email address you used to buy FeedFree. Your order number from the receipt helps, but isn't required.
  2. We'll reply and issue the refund, usually within 2 business days.
  3. The refund is processed by Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider. It goes back to the card or account you paid with; your bank usually shows it within 5–10 business days.

What happens to your license

A refunded license key is deactivated. FeedFree notices this at its next weekly check and stops hiding feeds; the sites then work as normal. You can remove FeedFree from Chrome at any time.

After 14 days

Refunds after 14 days are at our discretion. If FeedFree stopped working for you, for example because a site changed and we couldn't fix it, write to us and we'll help.
