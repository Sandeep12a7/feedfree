Break the scroll.

FeedFree is a Chrome extension that hides the endless feed on YouTube, Instagram, X, Reddit, LinkedIn and Facebook, and leaves a short quote in its place. Search, messages and everything you open on purpose keep working.

What it hides

Only the parts designed to keep you scrolling. Everything you came for stays.

  • YouTube

    Home page recommendations, the Shorts button and shelves, suggested videos beside a video, and suggestions when it ends.

  • Instagram

    The home feed and the Stories tray. Opening a story link shows a quote instead.

  • X

    The home timeline, the Trending panel and the news panel.

  • Reddit

    The home feed, r/all and r/popular. The subreddits you visit stay as they are.

  • LinkedIn

    The main feed and the news panel. Messages, jobs and profiles work as usual.

  • Facebook

    The News Feed. Messenger, groups and profiles work as usual.

You stay in control

FeedFree is meant to help, not nag.

  • Switch it on or off for each site, or everywhere at once
  • Show feeds for 5, 15 or 60 minutes; they hide again on their own
  • Hide feeds only on the days and hours you choose
  • Keep specific pages visible, like one subreddit or channel
  • An optional prompt that asks what you came for, with links to messages and search
  • A quote, your own quotes, or an empty space where the feed was
  • A quiet count of how many feed pages were hidden today
  • Export and import your settings

Private by design

FeedFree hides parts of these sites with CSS on your computer. It doesn't read, collect or send your posts, messages or browsing, and it has no analytics, tracking or ads. The only thing it ever sends is your license key, to the seller Lemon Squeezy, to check it's valid. Read the privacy policy.

Questions

What happens when the free trial ends?

FeedFree stops hiding feeds and the sites work as normal. Buy a license for $3 and paste the key into FeedFree's settings to keep hiding them.

How do I buy it?

Through Lemon Squeezy's checkout, linked from inside FeedFree. Lemon Squeezy handles payment and tax and emails you a license key.

I got a new computer. Do I pay again?

No. Open FeedFree's settings on the old computer and choose Deactivate this computer, then enter the same key on the new one. Your key is also in the receipt email from Lemon Squeezy.

A feed came back. What happened?

Sites change their layout from time to time. Tell us using Settings → "Something looks broken?" in FeedFree, or by email, and we'll ship a fix. Chrome installs updates automatically.

Can I get help or a refund?

Yes. Email vaskurisandeep@gmail.com from the address you bought with.
